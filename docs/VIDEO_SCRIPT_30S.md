# AstraHealth — 30-Second Demo Script

**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps
**Length:** 30 seconds
**Voice:** `en-US-AndrewNeural`, rate `+2%`, pitch `-2Hz`

Two cuts share the same 30-second body:

- `AstraHealth-Power-Astronaut-30s.mp4`
- `AstraHealth-App-Demo-30s.mp4`

## Shot list

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Title card: orbit mark, **AstraHealth**, “Mission-control software for the human body” | **A:** “AstraHealth. Presented by Power Astronaut.” **B:** “AstraHealth. A calmer way to check in.” |
| 0:03–0:10 | Flight Deck: readiness ring, six domains, factors, trend | On Mars, the flight surgeon is fourteen minutes away. AstraHealth turns the astronaut into the first line of care. |
| 0:10–0:18 | Body systems: 3D body, skin/muscle/skeleton layers, domain readouts | Six body systems. One explainable readiness score, built on sleep, heart-rate variability, and a personal baseline. |
| 0:18–0:21 | Systems Check: sensor-filled vitals, locked readings | The check-in fills itself from sensors. |
| 0:21–0:26 | Flight Surgeon workspace: early warning, space weather, alerts | The Flight Surgeon sees the same record, early warning, and cited guidance. |
| 0:26–0:30 | Outro: orbit mark, **AstraHealth**, “A calmer way to check in” | Guidance, not diagnosis. AstraHealth. |

## Why this cut works

- One astronaut, one problem, one loop: readiness → body detail → check-in → surgeon.
- The 3D body and the Flight Surgeon console get the most screen time after readiness.
- The voiceover never claims diagnosis or clinical validation.
- The branded cut adds only the team name; the app-only cut is identical otherwise.
